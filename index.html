A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.

Here's a little more about the residency, how it works and what to expect.

Where will the residency take place?

We're exploring destinations across Southeast Asia, including Vietnam, Thailand and Indonesia, as well as other suitable countries. The final destination will be selected based on accommodation quality, affordability, lifestyle, safety and connectivity.

What do you mean by premium but economical?

The goal is to enjoy a high quality of life without spending unnecessarily. We want a beautiful, comfortable home with good amenities and an enjoyable environment, while choosing a destination where accommodation and everyday expenses remain affordable.

How long will the residency last?

The duration of the first residency is being finalised. It will be based on the needs of the programme, participant availability and the practical arrangements.

Do I need technical or AI experience?

No. Participants arrive with different skills and experience levels. Curiosity and a willingness to learn are more important than knowing everything already. The programme is designed to encourage learning through practical exploration.

Who leads the learning programme?

The founder leads the learning programme and facilitates practical workshops, sessions and project activities. Participants also share their own skills and knowledge, creating opportunities for everyone to learn from one another.

Will we all work on the same project?

The residency includes both individual and group projects. Participants develop their own digital products based on their interests, while also collaborating on selected shared projects.

Who owns the digital products we create?

Individual projects belong to the participants who create them. Ownership and responsibilities for group projects are discussed and agreed upon before the project begins.

Is there a fixed daily schedule?

The residency follows a structured programme of learning and creation activities. The exact schedule is organised around the programme. Activities take place within agreed hours, leaving participants free to enjoy their own time outside those activities.

What would the contribution cover?

The contribution covers agreed shared living expenses such as accommodation, food and household costs. The final amount and inclusions are shared transparently before participants confirm their stay.

Will I need to bring anything?

A personal laptop is essential for taking part in the digital learning and creation activities. Other practical details will be shared ahead of the residency.

Is this a business or a startup incubator?

No. The central purpose is a shared living, learning and creation experience. The focus is on personal growth, practical skills, independent digital projects and community rather than operating a commercial production business.
