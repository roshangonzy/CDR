What if the place you live became the place where you grow?

The Digital Creation Residency is an idea for bringing together a small group of internationally minded people to live, learn and create in a beautiful destination. A home away from home, a space for exchanging knowledge, and an opportunity to explore what we're capable of creating.

Good people. New perspectives. Ideas worth exploring.

01 / The lifestyle

More than a place to stay.

Picture a beautiful villa in a destination that offers an excellent quality of life without an excessive price tag. A shared kitchen, comfortable bedrooms, a pool, good internet and spaces where people can work, relax or simply spend time together.

Some days might mean exploring the local area. Others might mean a long breakfast, a productive morning and an evening sharing stories over dinner.

The idea is to create a welcoming home where people feel comfortable being themselves. There is room for connection without the pressure to be together all the time.

  • A comfortable, beautiful shared home
  • Shared meals and everyday experiences
  • Time to explore and enjoy the destination
  • Space for privacy, rest and personal routines
  • A friendly international community
The destination

Premium living. Sensible spending.

Why limit ourselves to one country? The idea is to find destinations where our money goes further without compromising on the quality of the experience.

Think beautiful villas, comfortable shared spaces, good food, reliable internet and interesting places to explore — all at a cost that makes sense for everyone.

The destination hasn't been decided yet. We'll explore different possibilities and choose a place that offers the right balance of affordability, comfort, lifestyle and accessibility.

A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.

This is an early concept, so some details are deliberately open. Here's what we know so far.

Where will the residency take place?

The destination hasn't been decided yet. We're open to exploring different countries and locations. The priority is finding somewhere that offers an excellent balance of affordability, comfortable accommodation, lifestyle, reliable internet, safety and accessibility.

What do you mean by premium but economical?

The idea is to enjoy a high quality of life without spending unnecessarily. We want a beautiful, comfortable place with good amenities and an enjoyable environment, while choosing a destination where accommodation and everyday expenses remain affordable.

How long will it last?

That's still open. We're exploring different durations and would like to understand what works for people who are genuinely interested.

Do I need technical or AI experience?

Not at all. People will arrive with different skills and experience levels. Curiosity and a willingness to learn are more important than knowing everything already.

Will we all work on the same project?

Not necessarily. The idea is to give everyone space to develop their own digital products, while creating opportunities for collaboration where interests align. Any shared project will have its ownership arrangements agreed in advance.

Is there a fixed daily schedule?

The current proposal is approximately 20 structured hours per week, spread across four days. Activities would take place within agreed hours. Outside those hours, everyone is free to use their time as they wish.

What would the contribution cover?

The intention is to share accommodation, food and agreed household expenses. The final contribution and its inclusions haven't been set yet. Transparency around costs will be important.

Will I need to bring anything?

A personal laptop will likely be essential if you want to take part in the digital learning and creation activities. Other practical details will be shared once the first residency is planned.

Is this a business or a startup incubator?

No. The central idea is a shared living, learning and creation experience. The focus is on personal growth, practical skills, independent digital projects and community rather than operating a commercial production business.

Is everything already decided?

Definitely not. This is an exploratory first chapter. The idea is to gather honest feedback, understand people's interests and shape the experience before making final decisions.
